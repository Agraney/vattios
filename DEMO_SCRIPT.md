# VittaOS Executive Demo Walkthrough Script (4–5 Minutes)

This script provides a concise, high-impact walkthrough for executive presentations, founders, CFOs, and venture investors, mapping each screen directly to core business metrics.

---

### **Screen 1: Executive Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"This is the morning command center that replaces 6 fragmented spreadsheets, giving the CFO a real-time consolidated view of our ₹89.42 Lakhs cash balance, MTD revenues, and an automated 30-day cash flow forecast with 98.4% projection confidence."*
- **Business Metric:** Consolidates daily financial visibility from **3 hours of manual reporting into a single 10-second glance**.
- **Action:** Point to the 4 StatCards, the 6-Month Revenue vs. Expenses Area Chart, and the **30-Day Cash Flow Projection** (`+₹13.42L in vs -₹9.69L out`).

---

### **Screen 2: Invoicing & Revenue Operations (`/invoicing`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"Here, VittaOS automates GST-compliant e-Invoicing with instant IRN generation and automatic HSN tax rate determination, eliminating manual billing errors across ₹1.48+ Cr in wholesale shipments."*
- **Business Metric:** Cuts billing cycle times by **75%** and enforces **100% e-Invoice mandate compliance**.
- **Action:** Click the "Eye" icon on any invoice (e.g. `INV/2026-27/047` for Raymond Luxury Cottons) to display the verified line-item modal with tax breakdowns.

---

### **Screen 3: Accounts Receivable & Accounts Payable (`/ar-ap`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"In this module, our receivables aging schedules and vendor payables are unified with automated 1-click WhatsApp payment reminders and automated TDS withholding tracking under Sections 194C, 194J, and 194I."*
- **Business Metric:** Reduces Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) from **52 days to 38 days**, unlocking **₹20+ Lakhs in working capital velocity**.
- **Action:** 
  1. Click **"Send Reminder"** on an overdue invoice to show the instant WhatsApp/Email dispatch toast.
  2. Click **"View Match"** on a paid invoice to showcase the 100% matched bank UTR receipt.
  3. Switch to the **Payables** tab to highlight the inline **TDS Withholding Badges** (e.g. `TDS: ₹850 (Sec 194C @ 2%)`).

---

### **Screen 4: Bank Reconciliation (`/bank-reconciliation`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"This screen delivers on our core promise of cutting monthly bank reconciliation from 40 hours down to under 4 hours, boasting an 89.4% autonomous match rate against live HDFC feeds."*
- **Business Metric:** **90% reduction in reconciliation labor (40 hours → 4 hours)** with zero unposted journal variance.
- **Action:** 
  1. Point to the **89.4% Auto-Match Rate** banner.
  2. Click **"Confirm (✓)"** on a suggested match row (e.g. FabIndia Order Advance) to watch it instantly reconcile and post to the general ledger.
  3. Click **"Match manually"** on an unmatched row to demonstrate the interactive invoice/bill reconciliation picker.

---

### **Screen 5: General Ledger & Live Financial Statements (`/ledger`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"Because everything lives in one continuous double-entry ledger, P&L and Balance Sheet reports are live at any minute of any day — not a month-end export waiting on external accountants."*
- **Business Metric:** Eliminates the **15-day month-end financial close lag** into an **instantaneous, always-auditable real-time close**.
- **Action:** Switch between **General Ledger Entries**, **Chart of Accounts (COA)**, **Live P&L** (40.5% Gross Margin / 29.4% EBITDA), and the **Balance Sheet** (perfectly balanced to ₹0.00 variance).

---

### **Screen 6: GST Returns & Tax Compliance (`/gst-returns`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"This is the screen that compresses GST return filing from 3 days to 3 hours: it performs an autonomous 2-way reconciliation between our purchase ledger and GSTR-2B, catching vendor omissions before tax credit is lost."*
- **Business Metric:** **85% faster filing (3 days → 3 hours)** and **90% fewer ITC mismatch disputes**.
- **Action:** 
  1. Select the **Jul 2026** filing period to show the **₹19,000 ITC Discrepancy** with Gujarat Ambuja Cottons.
  2. Click **"Send Vendor Nudge"** to demonstrate automated vendor communication for Table 9 amendments.
  3. Point to the **4-Step Filing Readiness Checklist**.

---

### **Screen 7: Financial Copilot AI (`/copilot`)**
> **Talking Point:** *"Finally, Copilot gives the founder and CFO natural language intelligence over their entire ERP, allowing instant answers to complex liquidity, tax, and margin queries with verified ledger numbers."*
- **Business Metric:** Instant executive decision support with **zero SQL or accounting jargon**.
- **Action:** Click one of the suggested chips (e.g. *"What is our projected cash flow for the next 30 days?"* or *"Why did raw material expenses peak in June & July 2026?"*) to show the simulated neural thinking steps and the structured financial artifact card.
