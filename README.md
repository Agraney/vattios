## 🖥️ Screen & Route Directory

1. **Executive Command Center (`/dashboard`)**: Cash position (₹89.42L), MTD revenue (₹1.48 Cr), 6-month EBITDA trends, 30-day cash flow forecast, and priority attention queue.
2. **Invoicing & Revenue Operations (`/invoicing`)**: e-Invoicing with IRN/HSN, dynamic running balances, and chronological payment ledger audit drawer.
3. **Accounts Receivable & Payable (`/ar-ap`)**: AR aging schedules, 1-click WhatsApp reminders, settled bank UTR inspector, and AP TDS withholding badges (194C, 194J, 194I, 194Q).
4. **Autonomous Bank Reconciliation (`/bank-reconciliation`)**: Multi-tier matching against live HDFC statements with one-click general ledger auto-posting.
5. **General Ledger & Financial Statements (`/ledger`)**: Searchable double-entry register, 5-tier Chart of Accounts, Live P&L (29.4% EBITDA), and Live Balance Sheet (balanced to ₹0.00 variance).
6. **GST Returns & 2-Way ITC Reconciliation (`/gst-returns`)**: GSTR-1, GSTR-2B discrepancy detection, supplier nudges, and 4-step filing readiness check.
7. **Turnover Discount (TOD) Schemes (`/tod`)**: Volume & turnover incentive engine, qualifying invoice inspector, 20-step interactive demo simulator, and credit note posting.
8. **Financial Copilot AI (`/copilot`)**: Conversational ERP assistant with neural reasoning traces, interactive financial artifacts, and pre-built CFO prompts.
